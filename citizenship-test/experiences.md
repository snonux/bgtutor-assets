# What candidates say

First-hand reports from people who sat the exam, then second-hand opinions.
Most detailed reports are in Russian; they are summarised in English here.
Reddit and Facebook groups could not be searched during this research.

## First-hand reports

**2025, Sofia, May–June session** (author of bulgaria-dobrich.eu, second
attempt after 2022; post updated May 2026)
- 20 questions, 4 options, one hour, 12 needed, written only.
- Held at ПГ „Св. Евдокия". Most candidates were Turkish, others from Egypt,
  francophone Africa and Brazil.
- Examiners were stricter than in 2022 and the questions were "noticeably
  harder than the official samples". 20 people passed in that session.
- Tip: bring ID and a pen; the pen is not mentioned anywhere.
- <https://bulgaria-dobrich.eu/dokumentyi-dlya-bolgarii/yazyikovoy-ekzamen-dlya-polucheniya-bolgarskogo-grazhdanstva/>

**10 June 2025, Sofia** (mnogobukof.com)
- One main text, then synonyms, antonyms, idioms, degrees of adjectives,
  articles, agreement, spelling and punctuation, plus trick distractors (for
  example swapped units of measurement).
- Strict silence, individual seats, latecomers not admitted.
- Harder than her conversational level suggested: written accuracy is "a
  completely separate skill". Prepared with ChatGPT instead of a tutor.
- Results online after about 3 days; certificate sent by courier after she
  emailed her registration number and address.
- <https://mnogobukof.com/blog/gotovilas-ekzamenu-bolgarskomu-pomoshchyu-chatgpt/>

**2022, Sofia** (bulgaria-dobrich.eu, first attempt)
- A group of 31 (Russians, Syrians, Iranians, Belarusians). The test matched
  the official samples, the atmosphere was relaxed, she finished in about 30
  minutes. The hardest part was finding the building.

**2016–2023, bglife.ru forum thread** (<https://bglife.ru/topic/5044/>)
- 2016: no harder than the samples; one envelope for answers and one for your
  name, so marking is anonymous; phones off.
- 2019: idiom meanings and "find the error" were hard; guessed several. No
  oral commission.
- 2019: "very simple", low threshold. Only the MON certificate or a Bulgarian
  diploma counts, not course certificates.
- 2021 (COVID): applications by post only; test matched the samples, felt
  about A1; seats spread out. Registration number arrived by email after 1–2
  weeks.
- 2022–2023: the certificate does not expire and is not retaken when you file
  for citizenship.

**About 2017–18, expat.com** (<https://www.expat.com/forum/viewtopic.php?id=677122>)
- An intermediate speaker found it "really tough", with "quite archaic terms",
  idioms and fine synonym distinctions: "A lot of the Bulgarians around me
  couldn't correctly answer some of the questions!!!"

## Second-hand opinions

- expat.com, 2024–25: the test looks daunting but 12/20 is achievable; the
  real hurdle for weak speakers is the spoken interview at the ministry.
  Another member thinks drilling the sample formats is enough even without real
  proficiency.
  (<https://www.expat.com/en/forum/europe/bulgaria/1091105-question-bulgarian-citizenship.html>)
- Law firms put the level at A1 (Advocate Markov) or A1–A2 (bulgarian.llc).
  Sofia University's ДЕО sells an A2/B1 course for citizenship.
- Some sites (Language Magazine 2024, bulgarian-citizenship.com) claim the test
  covers history, culture and geography. The official samples and every
  first-hand report contradict this: the questions are purely linguistic.
- The Ministry of Justice interview is separate, in Bulgarian, and mostly
  personal questions (foreigner.bg; vesov.bg, June 2026).

## Patterns across the reports

1. The layout never changes; the difficulty has risen since about 2023.
2. Idioms and "find the error" questions cost people the most points.
3. Some vocabulary is literary or old-fashioned, so learn the idiom and synonym
   lists even if your everyday Bulgarian is good.
4. Logistics are simple but unforgiving: file on paper in time, arrive early,
   bring ID and a pen.
5. Pass rates in the official reports were about 70–78% (2018–2019).
