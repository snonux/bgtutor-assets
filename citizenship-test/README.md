# Bulgarian citizenship language exam: preparation guide

A guide to the Bulgarian language exam for citizenship by naturalisation
(изпит по български език за придобиване на българско гражданство по
натурализация). It was researched on 2026-10-03 from the law, the official
sample tests, the Ministry of Education's assessment centre, and first-hand
reports from people who sat the exam between 2016 and 2025. Every fact here has
a source in [sources.md](sources.md).

**Short version:** it is a 60-minute written multiple-choice test of 20
questions, and you pass with 12 correct. There is no essay, no oral part and
no civics or history section. The questions are always of the same seven
types, so targeted practice pays off more than general fluency. Recent
candidates (2025) say the real papers are harder than the 2013 sample tests,
so aim to score 17+ on practice tests before you book.

## 1. Who has to take it

The Bulgarian Citizenship Act (Закон за българското гражданство, ЗБГ), чл. 12,
ал. 1, т. 5 requires that a naturalisation applicant "владее български език"
and leaves the details to an ordinance of the Minister of Education.

- **You must take it** for ordinary naturalisation (чл. 12), usually after
  5 years of permanent residence (shorter for spouses of Bulgarian citizens,
  чл. 13), and as a stateless person (чл. 14).
- **You don't** if you apply as a person of Bulgarian origin, as someone
  adopted by a Bulgarian citizen, or with a Bulgarian parent (чл. 15), or under
  чл. 16 (special merit or state interest).
- **A 2023–2024 bill** would have made the language mandatory for people of
  Bulgarian origin too. It passed first reading on 21 February 2024 but the
  current law text (amended up to ДВ бр. 55 от 16.06.2026) still exempts them,
  so it never became law.
- **A Bulgarian diploma replaces the exam.** If you finished primary, secondary
  or higher education at a Bulgarian school, in Bulgaria or abroad, you file a
  copy of the diploma instead (Наредба № 5, чл. 2). Certificates from language
  schools and courses are not accepted on their own.

## 2. The exam itself

The legal basis is **Наредба № 5 от 03.09.1999 г. за реда за установяване
владеенето на български език при придобиване на българско гражданство по
натурализация**. It is run by the **Център за оценяване в предучилищното и
училищното образование (ЦОПУО)** of the Ministry of Education and Science
(МОН). The official page with dates and results is
<https://www.copuo.bg/node/600> (also <https://io.mon.bg/node/600>).

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | One written test, paper and pen |
| Length | 20 multiple-choice questions, 4 options (А, Б, В, Г), one correct; you circle the letter |
| Time | 60 minutes (most people finish in about 30) |
| Pass mark | 12 of 20 |
| Level | The ordinance says "нормална комуникация на елементарно ниво". No official CEFR level; people describe it as A2, with some literary vocabulary above that |
| Oral part | None. Blind candidates, people with disability group I–II and people over 70 get an oral interview instead |
| Civics or history | None. The reading texts are about Bulgarian places and people, but the questions only test understanding of the text |
| How often | Monthly, on dates announced in advance on the ЦОПУО page |
| Where | Sofia. Venues have moved; reports mention the National STEM Center at бул. „Драган Цанков" 21А and, in 2025, ПГ „Св. Евдокия". The ordinance also allows sitting it at a Bulgarian embassy or consulate |
| Results | Online by registration number, about 3 days after the exam (June 2025 report) |
| Certificate | Удостоверение за владеене на български език, collected from ЦОПУО or sent by courier. It does not expire |
| Retake | After 3 months (the 2015 ordinance text says 4; check the official page) |

### The seven question types

All three official sample tests and every first-hand report show the same
fixed layout:

| Questions | Type | Example from the official samples |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Reading comprehension of a short text (10–15 lines, sometimes with a table) | A news item about Бургас winning „най-добрият град за живеене"; a text on Васил Левски; Тетевен as a resort |
| 6–8 | Fill the gap with the right word form | „Кажи ми ………… са приятелите ти…" (кой / коя / кое / **кои**) |
| 9–11 | Which pair are synonyms? („В кой ред думите имат близки значения?") | съчувствие – състрадание |
| 12–14 | Which pair are antonyms? („…противоположни значения?") | изкачвам – слизам |
| 15–16 | Meaning of an idiom | „стоя на кръстопът" = двоумя се |
| 17–19 | Which word is misspelled? („В кой ред има грешка?") | посланици / военни / есенни |
| 20 | Which sentence has a punctuation error? | A vocative „скъпи гости" missing its second comma |

The distractors are built to catch people who match by sound: the wrong
options in synonym questions are often look-alike words (честен – частен,
разговор – договор), and the wrong options in antonym questions are often
synonyms.

## 3. Logistics

1. **Check the dates** on <https://www.copuo.bg/node/600>. The page may block
   some VPNs and foreign networks; try from a Bulgarian connection if it fails.
2. **Apply** with a written application (заявление) to the Minister of
   Education and Science, a copy of your ID card or passport signed „Вярно с
   оригинала", and any certificate of Bulgarian training you have. File it at
   ЦОПУО (бул. „Цариградско шосе" 125, бл. 5, ет. 2, Sofia) in person, by post
   or by courier, **not by email**, at least 3 days before the exam date (some
   reports say a week). The ordinance mentions a state fee, but several
   candidates and agencies report the exam as free; check the page.
3. You are told the **date, time, room and a registration number**.
4. **On the day** bring your ID and **your own pen** (a 2025 candidate stresses
   this). Arrive early: latecomers are not admitted. Phones are off, seating is
   spread out, and the paper is marked anonymously (one envelope for answers,
   one for your name).
5. **Results** appear online by registration number. Collect the certificate
   from ЦОПУО (room 209 in the reports) or email your registration number and
   address to have it couriered.
6. File the certificate with your citizenship application at the Ministry of
   Justice (Дирекция „Българско гражданство"). Later in the procedure there is
   an **interview in Bulgarian** at the ministry or consulate, mostly personal
   questions (why Bulgaria, work, family, where you live). That interview is
   separate from this exam, and several people say it is the harder part for
   non-fluent speakers, so practise talking about yourself too.

## 4. How to prepare

### What matters most

The exam rewards **exam-specific knowledge** more than conversation. People
who speak well have failed the idiom and spelling questions, and people with
limited speaking have passed by drilling the format. Because you only need
12, a sensible target is:

- Reading (5): get all five. These are the cheapest points.
- Gap fill (3): get all three. Gender, number, articles, pronouns.
- Synonyms and antonyms (6): get at least five.
- Idioms (2): learn the list; likely both.
- Spelling and punctuation (4): get two or three.

That is 16–18 with a margin for the hard questions.

### An eight-week plan (about 30–45 minutes a day)

Adjust to your level. If you are already around B1, compress weeks 1–4 into
two.

| Week | Focus | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Know the exam | Sit official sample test 1 cold, timed, and mark it. Read this guide and [experiences.md](experiences.md). Note which question types you lost |
| 2 | Grammar for 6–8 | Work through [study/grammar-checklist.md](study/grammar-checklist.md): gender, plural, the definite article (full and short), pronoun forms, numerals with masculine nouns (два стола, двама мъже) |
| 3 | Reading 1–5 | One short Bulgarian news item a day (BTA, БНР, Dnevnik). Answer: who, what, when, how many. Learn abbreviations: г., в., бул., ул., т.е., др., гр., с. |
| 4 | Synonyms and antonyms | [study/synonyms-antonyms.md](study/synonyms-antonyms.md), 20 pairs a day with flashcards; for each word also learn its opposite |
| 5 | Idioms | [study/idioms.md](study/idioms.md), 10 a day. Say each one in a sentence |
| 6 | Spelling and commas | [study/spelling-punctuation.md](study/spelling-punctuation.md). Copy out the example words by hand |
| 7 | Mock exams | [practice/](practice/) tests 1–3 and official samples 2–3, timed at 60 minutes. Review every mistake |
| 8 | Weak spots and the interview | Redo wrong answers; prepare a 2-minute spoken introduction of yourself for the ministry interview |

### Tactics in the exam room

- **Read the question first, then the text.** Questions 1–5 follow the order of
  the text. Watch numbers and tables (the Burgas text asked how many regional
  cities were ranked: 27, not the 29 criteria).
- **Abbreviations are fair game.** „в. „24 часа"" means вестник.
- **For gap fills, check agreement.** Look at the noun the gap refers to:
  gender, number and whether it is a person (двама щъркела is wrong; два щъркела
  is right because storks are not people).
- **For synonyms and antonyms, eliminate look-alikes.** If two words differ by
  one letter (разбран – разпран, кратък – кротък), they are almost always the
  distractor.
- **For "find the error", sound the word out by its root.** Ask what the
  related form is: посланик → посланици (one н), есен → есенен → есенни (два н).
- **Never leave a blank.** There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
- **Use the time.** You have an hour; go through everything twice.

## 5. Common pitfalls

- Expecting a conversation test and not drilling idioms and spelling.
- Assuming the 2013 samples are as hard as the real paper. In 2025 they were
  not.
- Confusing look-alike words in synonym questions.
- Adding a comma before „да" („Разбрахме се, да отидем…" is the error in sample 3).
- Forgetting the second comma around a vocative or an inserted phrase.
- Missing double н in adjectives (есенни, военни, каменна) and adding it where
  the root has only one (посланици, градинари).
- Writing „не" joined to a verb („немисля" is wrong; не мисля).
- Applying with only a language-school certificate.
- Arriving late or without a pen.

## 6. What is uncertain

The official ЦОПУО page could not be read during this research (it blocks
automated access), so these details come from candidates and agencies and
should be checked there before you apply: the 2026 exam dates, whether there
is a fee, whether the retake wait is 3 or 4 months, the current venue, and
what the post-2015 amendment to Наредба № 5 changed.
