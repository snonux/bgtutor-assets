# Grammar chapters for live classes

These chapters expand every rule in the grammar checklist and the supporting
grammar and punctuation patterns used by the citizenship materials. Each file
is one chapter about one rule. Work in this order unless a diagnostic identifies
a specific weak point; earlier chapters supply the prerequisites.

The teacher should begin with a question, introduce one example, let the learner
notice the pattern, practise together, then check independent use. Never read
the chapter or its private answer notes as a monologue. Pause after each prompt,
give a hint before an answer, and let the learner repair their sentence.

A chapter may take more than one 15–25 minute class. Check every exercise and
example family before recording mastery; correct use immediately after a model
is not enough. Revisit the rule in a later class. Spelling distinctions that sound
the same must also be shown or spelled in writing.
