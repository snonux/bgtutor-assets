# Idioms (фразеологизми) for questions 15–16

Questions 15 and 16 give an idiom and ask which of four options means the same.
The answer is usually a single verb or adverb (двоумя се, непременно) or a
short description of a person. Two came from the official samples: „тежи ми
на душата" and „стоя на кръстопът"; „на всяка цена" and „на мравката път
прави" are the others.

Learn the Bulgarian meaning, since that is what the options use.

## How to learn them

1. Cover the meaning column, read the idiom, say the meaning.
2. Make one sentence for each: „Цяла седмица ми тежи на душата, че не се
   обадих на мама."
3. Notice the trap pattern: the wrong options are usually the opposite feeling
   (радвам се vs. нещо ме измъчва) or a word that sounds related to the
   idiom's literal image.
