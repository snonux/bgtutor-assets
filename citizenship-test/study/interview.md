# A live rehearsal for the separate citizenship interview

The guide describes a later interview in Bulgarian, separate from the written
language exam. These are original rehearsal prompts, not an official question
bank or a guarantee of what an interviewer will ask.

### Tutor instructions

Conduct a short conversation as a teacher, then as an interviewer. Ask one
question at a time and wait. Start with a friendly introduction. Use only facts
the learner supplies; do not write a made-up life story for them. Follow their
answers with natural questions rather than reading the whole list.

1. Как се казвате? От коя страна сте?
2. Къде живеете сега? От колко време живеете в България?
3. С какво се занимавате? Как минава един Ваш работен ден?
4. Разкажете ми за семейството си.
5. Защо искате да придобиете българско гражданство?
6. Какво Ви харесва в живота в България?
7. Как учите български? Кога го използвате?
8. Какви са плановете Ви за следващата година?

### How to teach the answers

Let the learner finish. First respond to the meaning, then choose one useful
language correction. Ask them to improve that sentence and use the correction
in a new follow-up answer. Keep their own wording where it works. Offer a
sentence starter if stuck, such as „Живея в … от …“ or „Искам да …, защото …“.
Gradually remove these supports. Accept a truthful short answer before asking
for more detail. Never pressure them to share sensitive personal details.

### Independent rehearsal

Switch to a five-minute uninterrupted rehearsal with follow-up questions.
Afterward discuss clarity, understanding questions, sentence agreement, tense
choice and useful vocabulary. An AI assessment is teacher feedback, not an
official interview score. Record review until the learner can understand the
questions and answer spontaneously without reading a memorised script.
